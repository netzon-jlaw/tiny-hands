Tap sounds: original, synthesised for Tiny Hands. They contain no recorded or third-party audio
(no Kenney samples, no clips from other creators). Every file was generated by a Python/numpy
modal-synthesis script: damped resonant "plastic shell" modes, a short filtered-noise contact
click, a little band-passed noise, and an optional second contact or rattle.

Files: tap-01..10 (levels 1-2, ~110 ms), poke-01..10 (levels 3-4, ~140 ms, lower and firmer),
thwack-01..05 (level 5, ~230 ms, chunkier, with a short rattle). All are 44.1 kHz, mono, 16-bit,
normalised to -3 dBFS peak, 70 Hz high-passed (no DC offset) and given a 25 ms fade-out.

They are original work made for this project, with no third-party rights. The mode frequencies and decay times were
tuned against measurements of a small plastic hand tapping a screen. Those reference recordings
were only measured: no audio from them is included here.

These replace the earlier Kenney "Impact Sounds" (CC0) glass and soft-impact samples.
